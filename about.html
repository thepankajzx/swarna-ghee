Our Story & Vedic Philosophy

Room to roam. Time to graze. Care in every single detail.

THE SWARNA HERITAGE

A Life Closer To Nature

At Swarna in Himachal Pradesh, our journey started with a simple belief: pure food is the foundation of genuine health (Swarna). In a world dominated by industrial processing, chemical additives, and hybrid cattle, we chose to preserve the sacred connection between human wellness and indigenous Indian Gir cows.

Our cows graze freely under open skies and feed on fresh green organic fodder, dry grass, and traditional Ayurvedic herbs. Calves always receive their full share of mother's milk before any milking begins. We never use hormone injections or synthetic yield boosters.
